Metric definitions

Chamfer: mean of mesh→points and points→mesh distance, as % of the input bounding-box diagonal (lower is better).

F-score @τ: harmonic mean of precision (mesh samples within τ of a point) and recall (points within τ of the mesh), τ = 0.5 % / 1 % of the diagonal.

Colour PSNR: texture sampled at each input point's closest surface point vs that point's RGB.
